Privacy Policy

GTAVI Xtrackt · Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information GTAVI Xtrackt ("the App"), operated by Nei Maciel, Brazil, receives when a TikTok account is connected, and how it is used. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

1. Information we receive

When you connect your TikTok account through Login with TikTok, we receive only what is needed to publish videos to that account:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), from the user.info.basic scope.
  • An access token and a refresh token that allow the App to publish videos with the video.publish scope.

We do not collect passwords, contacts, messages, location, payment data or information about other TikTok users.

2. How we use it

The information is used only to authenticate with TikTok and publish the videos the App produces to your account, with the privacy setting you choose. The legal basis is the performance of the service you requested (LGPD, art. 7, V).

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only in a local configuration file on the operator's computer, which is not shared or uploaded to any other service. They are sent only to TikTok's API over HTTPS.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep the tokens while the authorization is active. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions), which invalidates the tokens. You can also ask us to delete any stored information by writing to darcinei@gmail.com. We delete it within 15 days.

6. Your rights

Under the LGPD you can request confirmation, access, correction, deletion and information about the processing of your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to darcinei@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly receive their information.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Contact: darcinei@gmail.com.
